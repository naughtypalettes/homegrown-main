Care and Resources Built for Creative Workers

Homegrown Arts & Media Collective is an artist-founded non-profit that supports the artist so they can make the art. From mentorship and career readiness to resource navigation, application help, and long-term well-being support.

We invest in the life behind the work.

The Homegrown path

A Sustainable Creative Ecosystem

A circular path of growth care.

  1. 01

    Care

    Access social services resources at no charge.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Use tried and tested templates and guidance.

  3. 03

    Build

    Gain hands-on experience to grow career readiness (SPARKS).

  4. 04

    Connect

    Build relationships with peers, mentors, funders and industry partners.

  5. 05

    Showcase

    A public platform that brings new work to Alberta audiences.

  6. 06

    Earn

    Fair, paid opportunities that help creative careers take root here.
