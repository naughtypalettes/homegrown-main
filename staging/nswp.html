The first project of its kind in Alberta.

Alberta's first embedded support program for the arts sector. Wellness that shows up where artists already work.

What NSWP is

Built by the sector, for the sector.

An embedded social worker who shows up where artists and arts workers already are: studios, rehearsals, festivals and offices. No referral, no waitlist, no need to be in crisis.

Right now we're listening. Before anything is built, artists and arts workers across Alberta tell us what would actually help.

How we work

Low-barrier. Trauma-informed. Decolonized.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

Something we didn't cover? Contact us.

Is it confidential?

Yes. The survey is anonymous, answers are shared as a group, and no one is quoted.

I don't have time.

It takes about 10 minutes. Or book a one-on-one instead and talk it through.

Surveys change nothing.

This one comes first. Your answers shape the program before it exists.

I'm not in crisis. Is this for me?

Yes. You don't have to be struggling to have a say.

I'm an arts administrator. Does this include me?

Yes. You hold the sector together, and you're allowed to need support too.

In distress right now?

NSWP isn't a crisis service. If you need help now, call or text 9-8-8, the Suicide Crisis Helpline, any time. In an emergency, call 9-1-1.
