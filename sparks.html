Mentorship built around the work

Your project sets the direction.

SPARKS meets emerging storytellers where they are. Instead of asking creators to fit a generic program, Homegrown connects each project with relevant guidance, practical structure and an experienced creative community.

The goal is simple: help strong ideas become stronger work—and help Alberta talent build momentum without leaving home to find opportunity.

Frequently asked questions

Before you apply.

Who is SPARKS for?+

Emerging storytellers, recent graduates and creators in roughly the first five years of their creative career. Applicants need a project they are ready to develop.

Do I need to live in Calgary?+

No. SPARKS is rooted in Alberta and remote participation is welcome, helping creators participate from across the province.

What kind of support can I expect?+

Mentorship is shaped around the project and may support planning, production and distribution—not a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

When do applications open?+

The mentee call is planned for October 2026. Join the application notice list to hear when dates and the form are confirmed.

Can I apply to be a mentor?+

Yes. Homegrown is building a cross-disciplinary pool of 10–15 mentors for the 2027 cohorts.
