Funding and partnerships

Fund the conditions where creative careers can grow.

Homegrown turns support into a connected creator pathway: learn, build, publish, connect and earn.

Build with us

Support shaped around real community needs.

Homegrown develops each funding opportunity with creators, partners and the communities the work is intended to serve. Program scope, timelines, budgets and outcomes are confirmed together before public commitments are made.

Creator-led

Support responds to the people doing the work.

Transparent

Roles, budgets and outcomes are agreed in advance.

Community-minded

Public value matters alongside partner goals.

Choose the right lane

Clear support. Clear expectations.

Funding, sponsorship and hiring serve different purposes. Homegrown keeps the agreements—and the promises—separate.

01

Public and foundation funding

Fund a cohort

Support structured learning, access, production and public presentation—with a clear outcomes framework and public-benefit reporting.

  • Cohort learning and access
  • Creator-controlled assets
  • Public showcase and reporting
02

Corporate sponsorship

Sponsor the platform

Support a program, event or access need through a defined commercial partnership with clear, values-aligned recognition.

  • Named program support
  • Defined visibility
  • Optional employee engagement
03

Hiring and procurement

Create paid work

Meet local talent through an open, opt-in pathway and commission creative work under a separate, fair-pay agreement.

  • No exclusive access
  • Fair creator pay
  • Creator ownership protected

What we measure

Public value and creator progress, together.

Partners should be able to see where support went and what changed—without reducing creators to marketing inventory.

  • 01Skills growth and creator-controlled work completed
  • 02Public reach, access and meaningful engagement
  • 03Paid opportunities, fees and repeat commissions
  • 04Regional reach and community resources shared
